# 02 — Product Principles

These are non-negotiable. Any change that violates one of these is wrong by default.

1. **Never hide opportunities.** Discovery is the product.
2. **Registration is optional.** Aggregator + academy work fully for guests.
3. **Search is always public.** No login wall on browse/search.
4. **Apply always redirects to source.** We never intermediate the application.
5. **No resume upload required** to browse or apply.
6. **No dark patterns.** No forced signups, no fake urgency, no bait.
7. **No ads.**
8. **Mobile first.** Every screen designed for phone first, then up.
9. **Fast.** Server-render public pages; keep client bundles lean.
10. **Accessible.** Keyboard, contrast, semantics, reduced-motion respected.
11. **Trust over volume.** A wrong title or mis-categorized row is worse than a missing
    one. Only verified, clean data is shown publicly.
12. **Worldwide, not India-only.** Schema and copy must never assume India.
